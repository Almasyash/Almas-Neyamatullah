MAHMAS GROUP is a premier software and design studio founded and operating directly in Bettiah, Bihar. We bridge the digital divide for local businesses.

原点 OUR STORY

Local Roots. Global Engineering Standards.

MAHMAS GROUP was founded in Chawanni, Bettiah with one clear purpose: why should local businesses in Bihar pay exorbitant fees to distant metropolitan agencies that don't understand our market or care about local post-launch support?

Led by full-stack developer Almas Neyamatullah, our studio engineers high-converting websites, fast billing software, and verified Google Business profiles specifically tailored to the real workflows of shops, clinics, coaching institutes, and hotels in West Champaran.

In addition to custom client software engineering, we actively operate MAHMAS Digital Cafe, providing direct digital services, printing, and technological enablement to local entrepreneurs.
