💻 Full Stack Developer 🎨 UI/UX Designer 📱 Mobile App Engineer 🏛️ Founder — MAHMAS GROUP

Almas Neyamatullah is a dynamic full-stack software engineer, mobile developer, and UI/UX designer based in Bettiah, West Champaran, Bihar. As the founder of MAHMAS GROUP (Government of India MSME Registered: UDYAM-BR-38-0075911), Almas architects high-performance digital platforms, offline counter POS systems, and native Android applications tailored to the practical needs of modern enterprises.

With technical mastery spanning front-end architectures, modern JavaScript, and Android engineering to backend business logic and Google Business optimization, he merges aesthetic discipline with rock-solid, production-ready code.

Beyond software architecture, Almas spearheads regional technology enablement through MAHMAS Digital Cafe, providing digital commerce tools, rapid printing, and IT guidance directly to local shops, clinics, coaching institutes, and entrepreneurs.
