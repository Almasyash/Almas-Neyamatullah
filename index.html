In-Depth Overview

A high-end, commercial-grade snack packaging design created for FS GROUPZ Gourmet Chips (Fiery Spice & Gourmet Herb). Crafted to maximize retail shelf impact, the design unites moody matte black aesthetics with fiery color gradients, metallic foil accents, and appetizing ingredient dynamics. From initial concept sketch and dieline precision to barcode compliance and print-ready CMYK separation, every detail was calibrated for commercial food packaging excellence.

Visual Highlights
✨
Shelf-Dominant Visual Hierarchy: Deep matte black canvas contrasting against vibrant fiery crimson and gold foil typography to captivate shoppers instantly.
🔥
Appetizing Flavor Narrative: Dynamic splash render of golden ridged crisps seasoned with cracked peppercorns, herbs, and Himalayan pink salt crystals.
🎯
Dual-Texture Material Finish: Engineered with tactile soft-touch matte film, spot UV gloss highlights on flavor imagery, and hot-stamped metallic gold branding.
📋
Certified Label Architecture: Standardized nutritional fact panels, allergen warnings, FSSAI compliance seals, batch barcodes, and net-weight metrics.
